At Urol, we celebrate the soul of Assamese cuisine.

From comforting dal chawal to our signature pork with dhekia saag, every bowl tells a story of tradition, local ingredients, and home-style cooking. We bring the authentic tastes of the Northeast to Raha, creating a space where modern dining meets rustic charm.

Local Joha Rice

Fragrant, indigenous rice sourced directly from local farmers.

Authentic Recipes

Traditional methods passed down through generations.

Fresh Ingredients

Using local Dhekia (fiddlehead fern) & Bhut Jolokia for that real kick.
